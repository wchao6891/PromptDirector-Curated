# Rights and sources

**English** · [简体中文](RIGHTS.md)

PromptDirector Curated is an editorially selected catalog of visual prompt references.

- Maintainers manually select original works, authorized works and references from third-party sources.
- Original and authorized works are recorded as `verified_original` and `verified_authorized`. Third-party works without evidence of redistribution permission are recorded as `source_unverified` and displayed as “Rights belong to the original author · Permission unverified”.
- Every public pack has a version-matched source and rights record, retaining the original author and source link for each case. Editorial approval is a selection decision; it does not substitute for permission from the rights holder or grant users commercial-use or redistribution rights.
- The published Original Chinese-Inspired Visuals collection was generated and manually reviewed by the PromptDirector project. The publisher retains generation and review records. Public access does not automatically grant rights to commercial republication, standalone resale or further public distribution.

If public content affects your rights, use the [English rights report](https://github.com/wchao6891/PromptDirector-Curated/issues/new?template=rights-report-en.yml) to provide the case name, source and rights details. We will investigate and suspend distribution or remove disputed content as appropriate.
